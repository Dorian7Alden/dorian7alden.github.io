## Daily English Dictation 91 - intermedia



### 🎧 Listening Practice



**My listening**:

```
My bad, tell me you have good insurance. Yeah, I've got. Allstate. Really?
```

**Answer**:

```
My bad. Tell me you have good insurance. Yep! I've got "Allstate". Really?
```
